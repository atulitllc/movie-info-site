Sultana is a drama set in Istanbul's one and only strip club, telling a story of a women, power, rivalry and solidarity. Meral the long reigning queen of the one and only strip club of Istanbul which is Sultana, takes a talented new Belarusian dancer named Anna under her wing. But she soon realizes she has created a rival powerful enough to replace her, and a ruthless power struggle erupts between the two women.
